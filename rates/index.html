Rates and rate plans.

Set by the Board of Public Utilities at public meetings, not a private board. Residential rates rise in tiers as usage climbs; commercial accounts choose a flat or time-of-use structure.

Residential rates.

Usage above the first block costs more per kilowatt-hour, which rewards conservation.

Usage Tier Price per kWh
Tier 1: first 500 kWh$0.1024
Tier 2: 501 to 1,000 kWh$0.1247
Tier 3: 1,001 kWh and up$0.1489
Fixed customer charge$9.50 / month

Current as of the July 2026 rate schedule. See Notices for the open Tier 3 rate case.

Commercial rates.

Small commercial accounts pay a flat per-kilowatt-hour rate. Accounts with demand over 20 kW are billed on a separate demand-charge schedule available from Customer Service.

Account Type Price per kWh Customer Charge
Small commercial (under 20 kW demand)$0.1198$18.00 / month
Demand accounts (20 kW and up)Call for scheduleVaries

Optional rate plans.

Any residential or small commercial account can switch, free of charge, once per calendar year.

Time-of-Advantage

On-Peak / Off-Peak

On-peak: weekdays 2 PM to 7 PM, $0.1690/kWh. Off-peak: all other hours, $0.0812/kWh. Best for accounts that can shift laundry, charging, and appliance use off-peak.

Billing

Equal Payment Plan

Averages your annual usage into one flat monthly amount, adjusted every 12 months. No seasonal spikes on a single bill.

Renewable

Green Power Choice

Add $4/month to match 100% of your usage with renewable energy credits from the Riverside Solar Array.
