Programs that lower the bill.

Seven programs cover efficiency upgrades, renewable energy, flexible billing, and help for customers who need it. Most are open to any residential account, no income requirement unless noted.

Efficiency rebates.

Rebate

Home Efficiency Rebate

Insulation, duct sealing, and heat pump upgrades qualify. A free home energy audit comes first and identifies which upgrades pay back fastest.

Up to $400 per household, per year
Rebate

ENERGY STAR Appliance Rebate

Refrigerators, clothes washers, and dishwashers with an ENERGY STAR rating qualify for an instant rebate at checkout at participating retailers.

$50 to $150 per appliance
Rebate

EV Charger Rebate

Install a Level 2 home charger and enroll in off-peak charging hours to qualify for a one-time rebate.

$250 one-time, per charger

Renewable energy.

Renewable

Riverside Community Solar

Subscribe to a block of the Riverside Solar Array without installing panels of your own. Credited straight to your bill each month.

$0.09 / kWh block locked for 5 years
Renewable

Green Power Choice

Add a flat monthly amount to match 100% of your usage with renewable energy credits, no equipment or subscription required.

$4 / month flat add-on

Billing & assistance.

Billing

Equal Payment Plan

Your bill is averaged across the year into one flat monthly amount, adjusted every 12 months. No seasonal spikes.

Same amount every month
Assistance

Low-Income Bill Assistance

Eligible households receive a monthly credit and are referred to state heating and cooling assistance funds. Applications are handled by Customer Service.

Income-based credit amount

Demonstration program details. Contact Customer Service for current eligibility and enrollment steps.
